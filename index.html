It's those little texts, your random replies, your presence, and the feeling of having a small part of your day shared with me that I genuinely enjoy. I don't expect you to be available all the time; I just wanted you to know that even your smallest gestures can mean a lot to me. That's one honest little broadcast from ATV. ♡

There was a time when moving forward didn't feel particularly easy. I was carrying more thoughts than I knew what to do with. And without making a big announcement or even trying to, you helped make things feel lighter. You reminded me that there can still be new smiles, new conversations, and genuinely good moments ahead. You deserve care, respect, peace, and a thousand little reasons to feel happy.

And if this year brings you unexpected turns, I hope it also brings the courage to take them, the strength to grow through them, and people who make the journey feel kinder. May every new chapter bring you something worth remembering. And whenever life gets a little too noisy, I hope you never forget that you deserve a little peace, a little kindness, and a whole lot of happiness.
